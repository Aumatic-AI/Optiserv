## What it is

Optiserv Consulting builds and runs **dedicated offshore teams** for companies in the UK, USA,
Australia and Canada, delivered from **Hyderabad, India** and **Cebu, Philippines**.

The unique mechanism in one sentence: *a client gets a named, dedicated team member for a flat
monthly fee, and Optiserv absorbs every cost that normally surrounds that hire — recruitment,
HR, payroll, office seat, equipment, IT and day-to-day management.*

## The offer

- Dedicated team members **from $1,500 per month**
- **30-day trial** to test vetted people (exact terms TO CONFIRM)
- Included: recruitment, HR and payroll, office seat, equipment, IT support, account manager
- Automation services scoped per project

## Audience

Founders, CEOs, MDs, COOs, CTOs and HR heads in the **UK, USA, Australia and Canada**.
They are buying relief from headcount overhead, not "resources." They are time-poor,
sceptical of offshore quality, and worried about timezone friction and hidden costs.

## Services

1. **Dedicated staffing** — engineers, QA, design from India and the Philippines
2. **Operational staffing** — HR, payroll, infrastructure, team management
3. **Recruitment & RPO** — Full-Cycle RPO, Project RPO, Recruiter On Demand
4. **Automation services** — scoped per project
