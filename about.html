About Optiserv

We take the overhead, you keep the team.

Optiserv Consulting builds and runs dedicated teams for companies in the UK, the US, Australia and Canada, delivered from Hyderabad and Cebu. We employ the people, so you do not have to.

Who we are

Hiring abroad is rarely blocked by a shortage of good people. It is blocked by everything attached to employing them — an entity, a payroll, a contract that holds up, a desk, a laptop, an IT policy, and somebody senior enough to notice when things drift.

Optiserv exists to carry that part. We source and vet the people, employ them on our books in India or the Philippines, give them a seat and the equipment to do the job, and put an account manager over the engagement. What reaches you is a named person who turns up to your stand-up and works on your product.

We also run recruitment as a standalone service for roles you want on your own payroll, and we automate work where automating it is plainly better than staffing it.

Why companies pick us

  • You direct the work, we employ the people. No agency margin on a body you still have to manage yourself.
  • Two delivery centres, one contract. Hyderabad and Cebu give you a genuine choice of timezone and cost base.
  • Shift hours are a decision, not an excuse. We staff to your working day, including the evening shifts that make a US overlap real.
  • A 30-day trial before you commit. If the person is not right, that is our problem to fix, not yours to absorb.
  • Scale down as easily as up. The contract flexes with the team rather than against it.
