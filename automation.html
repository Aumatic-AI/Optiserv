How a project is scoped

Automation is priced per project rather than per seat, because the value is in the thing built, not the hours spent building it.

  1. Observe. We watch the process as it actually runs, not as the documentation describes it.
  2. Qualify. Volume, variability and stability. A process that changes every month is usually not ready to automate.
  3. Scope and price. A fixed scope with a fixed price, and an explicit statement of what is out of scope.
  4. Build and hand over. Documented, in your repository, with your team able to maintain it.
