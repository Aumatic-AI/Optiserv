Operational staffing

The functions around the product.

HR, payroll, infrastructure and team management, staffed as a unit. The work that keeps a growing company running, and that usually lands on whoever has least time to refuse it.

Talk it through

What we cover

Staffed roles, not a managed service you have no visibility into.

HR

People operations

Onboarding, records, leave, performance paperwork and the day-to-day questions that otherwise reach a founder.

Finance

Payroll administration

Payroll runs, statutory filings and the reconciliation around them, handled by people who do it every month.

Technology

Infrastructure and IT support

Device provisioning, access management, helpdesk and the security hygiene that audits ask about.

Delivery

Team management

Team leads and coordinators who hold a distributed group together, run the rituals and escalate early.

When this makes sense

  • Your engineers are spending real hours on IT, access and onboarding admin.
  • Payroll and HR are being run by somebody whose job title says something else.
  • You are opening a second location and the operational load is about to double.
  • You have the headcount budget but not the management bandwidth to supervise it.
