Cookie Policy

What this site stores on your device, and how to change it.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are required for a site to function; others help the owner understand how the site is used.

What we use

Essential. We store your cookie choice locally so that we do not ask again on every page. This is set only once you choose, and it carries no identifier.

Analytics. If you accept analytics, we load an analytics provider so we can see which pages people read and where they arrive from. It is never loaded unless you accept it, and nothing is used to identify you personally or to target advertising.

Your choice

You are asked once, on your first visit, and your choice is remembered. You can change it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser, which will cause the notice to appear again.

No analytics is loaded unless you accept it.

Browser controls

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking essential storage may mean the cookie notice reappears on each visit.
