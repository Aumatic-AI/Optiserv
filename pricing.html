What the monthly fee covers

  • Recruitment and vetting. Sourcing, screening and shortlisting for the role, at no separate placement fee.
  • Employment, salary and statutory payroll. The person is on our books, in our entity, under local employment law.
  • HR and compliance. Contracts, records, leave and the paperwork that comes with employing someone.
  • Office seat, power and connectivity. A real desk in Hyderabad or Cebu, not a home-broadband gamble.
  • Laptop, peripherals and licences. Provisioned and maintained by us.
  • IT support and device security. Access management, helpdesk and security hygiene.
  • An account manager. One named person responsible for the engagement and for raising problems before you notice them.

The 30-day trial

Thirty days to work with the people we put in front of you, on real work, inside your own tools, before the engagement becomes a commitment.

  • They start properly. Seat, laptop, accounts and access on day one — not a trial period spent waiting for a login.
  • They work on your backlog. Not a sample task. The trial is only useful if it tests the actual job.
  • Your account manager checks in. Twice inside the thirty days, with a written note on how it is going.
Thirty days is long enough to know.
Long enough to see how someone handles a real ticket, a code review and a disagreement — which is what you are actually buying. If it is not working, say so and we will deal with it. The exact terms are set out in your contract before you commit to anything.
