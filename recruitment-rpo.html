Recruitment & RPO

Hiring run as a process, not a scramble.

For roles you intend to put on your own payroll. We take the sourcing, screening and scheduling, and hand you a shortlist worth interviewing.

Discuss a hiring plan

Three engagement models

Pick the one that matches how much of the process you want to keep.

Model 01

Full-cycle RPO

We own the hiring process end to end — workforce forecasting, sourcing, screening, scheduling, offer management and onboarding.

Model 02

Project RPO

A dedicated recruiting team stood up for a defined push: a funding round, a new market, a product launch. It ends when the hiring does.

Model 03

Recruiter on demand

One or more recruiters added to your existing talent team for a specific discipline or a specific stretch.

What outsourcing the process changes

  • Cost per hire. No per-role agency placement fee. Sourcing cost is absorbed into the engagement rather than charged again on every hire.
  • Time to hire. A team working one brief, rather than a contingency recruiter splitting attention across a dozen open roles at other companies.
  • Candidate experience. Scheduling, feedback and offer communication stop slipping, which is where most good candidates are actually lost.
  • Quality of shortlist. Structured screening against a written brief, so you interview four people rather than fourteen.
  • Scalability. Hiring volume can double for a quarter without you hiring recruiters to hire people.
  • Employer brand. Consistent, prompt communication with every candidate, including the ones you reject.
